Services / B2B lead generation service

What’s included

A managed outbound workflow from targeting to qualified conversations. Simple for the client, structured behind the scenes.

ICP and targeting

ICP means your ideal customer - the type of company and person most likely to buy from you. We define exactly who to target - role, company size, market, location - to get the best leads for your business.

Lead sourcing and enrichment

Lists built from LinkedIn, Sales Navigator and market data, enriched with recent posts and company profile context - so every message has something real to work with.

Personalization at scale

We use Python scripts and AI to pull real facts about each company and turn them into a message that actually fits - not a generic template with a name swapped in.

LinkedIn and email outreach
1,000+unique personalized touches every month
36%connection acceptance rate we aim for

A coordinated sequence of LinkedIn and email follow-ups running over several weeks, built to turn familiarity into qualified conversations.

How it works

One coordinated system: focused research first, then consistent follow-up across LinkedIn and email.

01
SetupTargeting and research

We define the ICP and research each prospect using company profiles, recent posts and relevant market context.

02
SetupLinkedIn launch

We warm up the contact, send a personalized connection request and open the conversation with a relevant message.

03
Core sequenceMulti-step follow-up

A structured sequence runs over several weeks, with 400–1,000 LinkedIn messages per month including follow-ups. Each message adds context instead of repeating the same pitch.

04
Second channelEmail outreach

Email runs alongside LinkedIn to reach prospects who did not respond there. Dedicated domains and mailboxes are warmed before launch to protect deliverability.

05
OutcomeQualified replies and meetings

Interested prospects move into real conversations, qualification and meeting booking.

Why campaigns fail

×
The list is too broad

Big list, no buying context. Activity goes up, qualified replies stay low.

×
The message feels generic

Decision makers get dozens of these a day. If it's not specific to them, it's ignored.

×
The follow-up is weak

Most replies don't come from message one. No consistent follow-up, no result.
